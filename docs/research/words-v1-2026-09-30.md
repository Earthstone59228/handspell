# Word signs v1: PopSign spike (2026-09-30), not shipped

**Question.** Can Handspell recognise common ASL word signs on the device, using the same MediaPipe hand
landmarker as the letters?

**Data.** PopSign ASL v1.0 (CC BY 4.0), non-game subset, from the `sign/popsign-images` Hugging Face mirror:
244 glosses. Each sample is the sign segment as 256x256 frames at about 5 fps, with a median of 5 frames per
sign. The splits are by participant: 25,419 train samples, and 10,798 held-out samples (validation + test).

**Method.**
- `training/scripts/extract_popsign.py` extracts the hand landmarks.
- `training/scripts/train_words.py` builds the features: 8 resampled steps, two hand slots, spec-v1 shape,
  wrist position and presence, plus dominant-wrist motion. It trains an MLP with layers 256 and 128.

**Results on held-out participants.**

| Model | top-1 | top-3 | drill true-accept (p >= 0.05 and target in top 3) | false-accept |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 244 words | 0.188 | 0.302 | 0.28 (the 12 free words: 0.19) | 0.008 |
| 12 free words only | 0.518 | 0.693 | — | — |

In the 12-word model, **hello, thank you, happy, hungry, mom and dad score 0.00**. Every one of them is signed
at or near the face.

**Diagnosis.**
- Only about 55% of frames contain a detected hand, and two hands are almost never detected together.
- At 5 fps and 256 px, the palm detector loses hands in front of the face. It also loses them at the frame edge,
  where the pose segmentation often cuts the sign.
- The data is too sparse and too low-resolution for handshape + location + motion recognition. More samples of
  the same quality will not fix face-located signs.

**Decision.** Words are **not** in the Shipaton build. Next attempt:
1. Use full-frame-rate source video or landmarks. The candidates are the original PopSign videos, or Google's
   ISLR landmark set once its licence is confirmed in writing. Include face or pose landmarks so that location
   relative to the face is explicit.
2. Use a sequence model (a 1D conv or a small transformer) instead of a fixed 8-step MLP.
3. Validate with the same drill gate metrics: true-accept against false-accept.
