# Stage-2 MLP (mlp-v1) — 29 September 2026

**Result:** the app now ships a trained MLP (`assets/classifier/mlp-v1.bin`). On live poses of a letter the model never saw live, it passes the match gate on about 53% of single frames. Averaged over all wrong targets, false accepts stay at or below 2.4%, but look-alike targets can be far higher (see False accepts by target). The previous k-NN passed 0% for E–Y. A–D do not regress.

## Why E–Y failed

E–Y references came only from ASLYset still photos. Live camera poses sit a median distance of 0.62 from them, while the k-NN match gate allows 0.32. Against photo-only references, live A–D poses score 43% top-1 and about 1% gate pass (`training/scripts/evaluate_live_gate.py`). A–D worked because team-recorded live references exist for them. Mirroring and axis flips were ruled out, since every flip made accuracy worse.

## Recipe (`training/scripts/train_mlp.py`)

- Data: 4,786 ASLYset photo vectors (4 signers, 24 letters) + 98 team live vectors (A–D).
- Domain shift: about 72% of the live-minus-photo offset is shared across A–D. It is concentrated at the fingertips (x and z) and in orientation feature f60. The mean offset is added to every photo vector.
- Live rows repeated 8×; 4 noisy copies of everything (landmark σ 0.07, orientation σ 0.15, scale ±10%).
- Model: 66 → 64 (ReLU) → 24, sklearn `MLPClassifier`, α = 1e-3, seed 0.
- App gate unchanged: target probability ≥ 0.85 and margin ≥ 0.20. Stage 2 has no distance check.

## False accepts by target

"False accept" means that a pose of letter Y passes the gate while the drill target is X. Averages hide look-alikes, so here is the worst pair in each test (Codex T15 flagged this):

| Test | Worst pairs | Pairs above 10% |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shipping recipe, live A–D, 5-fold | B→F 0.067; all other pairs 0 | 0 of 92 |
| Photo, leave-one-signer-out | R→U 0.375, T→S 0.205, S→T 0.07 | 2 of 552 |
| Leave-one-letter-out, letter with no live data (proxy for live E–Y) | A→S up to 0.54; other folds' worst 0.34–0.50 | several |

The shipped model has live data for A–D, so its A–D look-alikes are low. E–Y have no live data, so live E–Y may be confused with neighbours (for example the fist group A/E/M/N/S/T) at rates like the last row. R/T/U already show the in-app experimental note, and S should arguably get it too.

Independent check (Codex T15, `training/scripts/verify_mlp_lolo.py`): it reproduced 0.531 / 0.516 / 0.537 across seeds and found no held-out-letter leakage. The recipe was still chosen on the same four folds, so some selection bias remains. JVM parity between `mlp-v1.bin` and numpy holds to 1e-4 (`MlpAssetParityTest`).

## Limits

- No live E–Y data exists. The leave-one-letter-out number is the closest proxy, and D shows it can be much lower for some letters.
- Live data comes from the team's A–D signers only. Other people's hands are untested live.
- These are per-frame rates. The app smooths over frames and needs a hold, so a 50% per-frame rate usually still confirms within a short hold, but that has not been measured.
- R/S/T/U remain weak even within the photo domain. The in-app experimental note stays.
- Personal calibration (Settings) feeds the stage-1 k-NN only. With the MLP loaded, check whether calibration still affects recognition (T15).
