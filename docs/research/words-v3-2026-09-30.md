# Word signs v3: ISLR landmarks + conv network (2026-09-30)

Supersedes `words-v1-2026-09-30.md` (PopSign 5 fps frames, failed).

**Data.** Google's Kaggle ISLR (`asl-signs`), CC BY 4.0: 94,477 MediaPipe Holistic landmark sequences, 250 signs, 21
signers, full frame rate. Used: all sequences of the 12 free words (`hello, bye, thankyou, yes, happy, sad, hungry,
drink, water, mom, dad, home`, about 390 each) plus 60 sequences each of 34 unrelated signs as an "other" class.
Only hand, nose and shoulder points are kept (the phone has the Hand Landmarker plus the Pose Landmarker lite).

**Features** (`training/handspell/words_features.py`, ported to `WordFeatures.kt`, golden-tested). Crop to the frames
that contain a hand; two hand slots assigned by presence (no handedness labels); hand shape relative to the wrist and
hand size; wrist and fingertips relative to the nose in shoulder-width units; 16 resampled steps. Training adds
left-right mirrored copies, so left- and right-handed signing both work.

**Model** (`words_net.py`, `WordNet.kt`, golden-tested): per-step embed, three 1D convolutions, mean+max pooling, a
small head. 1.9 MB. Trained by `scripts/train_words_conv.py` (about 1 minute on the laptop GPU).

**Result, signers the model never saw** (3 folds of 7 signers, mirror averaging on, gate p >= 0.5):

| | top-1 (12 words) | drill true-accept | drill false-accept | worst word false-accept |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| mean of 3 folds | 0.854 | 0.831 | 0.005 | 0.013 |
| per fold | 0.769 / 0.892 / 0.901 | 0.733 / 0.875 / 0.886 | 0.006 / 0.004 / 0.004 | |

Weakest words across folds: thankyou 0.65 (fold 0), hello 0.69 (fold 1), sad 0.72 and bye 0.73 (fold 2). The exported model
is trained on all 21 signers, so it has 7 more signers than each fold's model.

**Not yet measured (honest limits).**
- All of this is ISLR-to-ISLR. The phone's Hand and Pose landmarkers differ from Holistic, and the phone feeds live camera
  frames in a rolling window, so on-device accuracy is unmeasured. A wrong hand/pose convention would show up as a big
  drop; the mirror-invariant training is meant to absorb the flip convention only.
- Samples in which no hand was detected at all (about 7% of the free-word clips) are dropped from training and scoring.
- "Other" is 34 signs, not all of ASL; an unrelated sign outside it can still be mistaken for a word.
- The 21 ISLR signers are a small, specific group, so accuracy for other signers may differ.

Reproduce (from `training/`): `kaggle competitions download -c asl-signs -p data/islr/bulk` (37 GiB, one request; per-file
downloads are throttled to a few files a minute), unzip `train.csv` into `data/islr/`, then `scripts/extract_islr.py --zip
data/islr/bulk/asl-signs.zip` for the free words and for the other signs (`--per-sign 60`), then `scripts/train_words_conv.py`.
