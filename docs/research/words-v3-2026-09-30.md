# Word signs v3: ISLR landmarks + conv network (2026-09-30)

Supersedes `words-v1-2026-09-30.md` (PopSign 5 fps frames, failed).

**Data.** Google's Kaggle ISLR (`asl-signs`), CC BY 4.0: 94,477 MediaPipe Holistic landmark sequences, 250 signs, 21
signers, full frame rate. Used: all sequences of the 12 free words (`hello, bye, thankyou, yes, happy, sad, hungry,
drink, water, mom, dad, home`, about 390 each) plus 60 sequences each of 34 unrelated signs as an "other" class.
Only hand, nose and shoulder points are kept (the phone has the Hand Landmarker plus the Pose Landmarker lite).

**Features** (`training/handspell/words_features.py`, ported to `WordFeatures.kt`, golden-tested). Crop to the frames
that contain a hand; two hand slots assigned by presence (no handedness labels); hand shape relative to the wrist and
hand size; wrist and fingertips relative to the nose in shoulder-width units; 16 resampled steps. Training adds
left-right mirrored copies, so left- and right-handed signing both work.

**Model** (`words_net.py`, `WordNet.kt`, golden-tested): per-step embed, three 1D convolutions, mean+max pooling, a
small head. Trained by `scripts/train_words_conv.py` (about 1 minute on the laptop GPU).

**Two models.** One 36-way model costs the free words about 4 points, so the words are split by tier, each model with its
own "other" class (which includes the other tier's words, so a free-word sign cannot trigger a Pro word or the reverse):

| model | classes | top-1 | drill true-accept (p >= 0.5) | drill false-accept | size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `words-v3.bin` (free) | 12 words + other | 0.850 | 0.826 | 0.005 | 1.9 MB |
| `words-pro-v3.bin` (Pro) | 24 words + other | 0.837 | 0.806 | 0.003 | 1.9 MB |

Both are 3-fold cross-validated by signer (7 unseen signers per fold, mirror averaging on); the shipped files are trained
on all 21 signers. Pro words were chosen from 40 candidates: the 24 shipped
are those with the best held-out recall (bird, uncle, horse, cow, cry, sick, boy, girl, aunt, read, talk, red, black,
wake, now, think, tomorrow, yellow, green, mad, morning, brother, grandpa, cat). Words such as go, give, look, night, dog,
sleepy, white and hate were dropped because they recognised at 0.15 to 0.55 on unseen signers. On the hardest fold the weakest Pro
words were brother (0.49), mad (0.61), uncle (0.62) and green (0.63); in an earlier run of the free setup thankyou, sad, bye
and hello were the weakest (0.65 to 0.77).

**One-handed signing.** In ISLR the second hand is tracked in about 1% of frames, even for signs that are two-handed in
everyday ASL (happy, sad, read): the signers used one hand. So the model only ever sees the dominant hand (the second
hand's features are zeroed, in Python and Kotlin), the reference animations show one hand, and the app tells the learner
plainly to sign with one hand. The word signs taught are therefore one-handed versions of some signs.

**Reference animations** (`scripts/build_word_refs.py` -> `assets/content/word-refs.json`): per word, the ISLR sequence
closest to the word's mean among clean, consistent-side signers, gap-filled, smoothed, resampled to 20 frames, mirrored to
match the selfie preview and drawn in one fixed body frame. Free-word tips in `words.json` are Claude's descriptions and
still need a check against a Deaf-led source.

**Not yet measured (honest limits).**
- All of this is ISLR-to-ISLR. The phone's Hand and Pose landmarkers differ from Holistic, and the phone feeds live camera
  frames in a rolling window, so on-device accuracy is unmeasured. A wrong hand/pose convention would show up as a big
  drop; the mirror-invariant training is meant to absorb the flip convention only.
- Samples in which no hand was detected at all (about 7% of the free-word clips) are dropped from training and scoring.
- "Other" is about 50 signs (33 unrelated signs plus the other tier's words), not all of ASL; an unrelated sign outside it can still be mistaken for a word.
- The 21 ISLR signers are a small, specific group, so accuracy for other signers may differ.

Reproduce (from `training/`): `kaggle competitions download -c asl-signs -p data/islr/bulk` (37 GiB, one request; per-file
downloads are throttled to a few files a minute), unzip `train.csv` into `data/islr/`, then `scripts/extract_islr.py --zip
data/islr/bulk/asl-signs.zip` for the free words and for the other signs (`--per-sign 60`), then `scripts/train_words_conv.py`.
