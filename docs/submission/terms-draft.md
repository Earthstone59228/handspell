## Using Handspell

Handspell is a student hackathon app for practicing ASL fingerspelling. It is a practice aid, not an ASL authority, teacher, or certification tool. Recognition can be wrong. J and Z use experimental motion recognition, and recognition for R, T, and U is experimental.

The free app offers camera drills for static letters, 12 word signs (taught one-handed), alphabet and word checkmarks, a daily quest, a daily speed challenge and local progress. Optional Pro access adds story packs, unlimited speed challenges, 24 more word signs and progress insights. A separate 3-day demo trial of the free-trial flow runs locally on your phone; it is not a store trial and never becomes a purchase. You need to grant camera permission to use camera drills. Practice content is bundled with the app; there is no account or backend.

## Pro and test purchases

In this hackathon build, Pro is unlocked through RevenueCat's Test Store. Test Store subscriptions and purchases are simulated. They do not charge real money or create a paid subscription. RevenueCat checks whether the app has a Pro entitlement. A purchase or restore result may depend on the Test Store service being available.

This prototype notice does not set terms for real-money purchases. If the app later uses live billing, its operator must publish appropriate purchase terms before offering that billing.

## Your data

Camera processing runs on your phone. The app does not save or upload camera frames. Practice progress is saved locally and can be deleted in Settings. RevenueCat receives information needed for the test purchase and entitlement flow. Read the [privacy notice](../PRIVACY.md) for details; deleting local practice data is separate from any request about data held by RevenueCat.

## Privacy notice gaps to resolve

The current privacy notice already explains on-device camera processing, that camera frames are not saved or uploaded, what local progress is stored, how to delete both local progress stores, and the kinds of purchase and entitlement data sent to RevenueCat. It still needs:

- Confirm the maintainer contact is monitored and establish a private channel for privacy requests.
- A clear way for users to request access to or deletion of purchase and entitlement data held by RevenueCat, including who will handle the request. The current **Delete practice data** control is described only for local progress.
- Owner-confirmed terms for how RevenueCat-held data is retained or deleted. Do not state a retention period until confirmed with RevenueCat.
- An explanation of whether uninstalling the app affects locally stored progress or RevenueCat-held purchase data, so users can distinguish device deletion from a RevenueCat data request.
